Notes For Interpretation In The Write-Up

Do not describe AUC as the only measure of success.

Use this framing:

AUC evaluates whether the treated and control risk models can rank ED risk.
Calibration and Brier score evaluate whether predicted probabilities are numerically believable.
Uplift deciles evaluate whether the model can prioritize members by expected intervention benefit.
Observed treated-control gaps by decile provide directional validation of the uplift ranking.
ROI summarizes the potential business value under the stated cost assumptions.

Sensitivity and specificity are optional appendix metrics. They are less central because the project is not mainly a binary ED classification problem; it is an uplift/benefit-ranking problem.
